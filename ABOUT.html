About Shikaku

A number-logic puzzle game published by Tunaroz

Our game

Shikaku is a logic puzzle game built around numbers, rectangles, and careful reasoning. The goal is to solve each puzzle by dividing the grid into rectangles according to the numbers shown.

Shikaku is designed for players who enjoy focused problem-solving and a calm puzzle experience.
