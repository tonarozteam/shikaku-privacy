Privacy Policy

Shikaku — a number-logic puzzle game published by Tunaroz

Policy review date: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tunaroz ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the Shikaku mobile game ("Shikaku" or the "Game"). It should be read together with the data practices of the services actually included and enabled in the version you use.

1. Information and gameplay data

Shikaku is a puzzle game. Game progress and settings may include level progress, scores, completion times, settings, virtual currency, hints, and other gameplay state. The precise fields stored and whether any data is backed up or transmitted depend on the version and platform configuration. Do not assume that uninstalling the Game deletes information already retained by third-party providers.

If you contact support, we receive the information and contact details you choose to include in your message. Please do not send passwords, payment credentials, or unrelated sensitive information.

2. Advertising and Google services

Shikaku has been developed with Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) integrations in mind. The exact SDK versions, enabled ad formats, configuration, and data flows must be assessed against the current production build. When Google advertising services are active, Google and its partners may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers where available, app and device information, ad delivery or interaction events, and signals used to prevent fraud or abuse. The precise data and purposes depend on the SDK configuration, device settings, region, and applicable consent or privacy choices.

Where a consent or privacy-options interface is presented, its availability and behavior depend on the app's UMP configuration and applicable requirements. This policy page is separate from any in-app consent or privacy-options controls.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The Game is built with Unity. Additional packages or service providers may be included in a particular version. We do not identify analytics, crash-reporting, attribution, or other telemetry services here as confirmed data collection unless they have been verified in the exact version. Depending on the verified services active in the version you use, information may be processed to operate and save gameplay, serve and measure ads, respond to support messages, protect against invalid activity, or maintain technical functionality. Gameplay data may be stored on the device. Its exact location, backup behavior, and deletion behavior depend on the implementation and Android settings. Retention periods for provider-collected information are described in the relevant providers' policies. Support correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to handle the request and maintain appropriate records.
