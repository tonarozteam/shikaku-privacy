Privacy Policy

Shikaku — a number-logic puzzle game by Tunaroz

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tunaroz (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in the Shikaku mobile game (“Shikaku” or the “Game”). Please read it before using the Game.

1. Information the Game may process

The Game is designed to provide puzzle gameplay. Depending on the version, features you use, your device, and the services enabled, the following information may be processed:

  • Gameplay and local progress: level progress, scores, completion times, settings, coins, hints, and other gameplay preferences. These may be stored locally on your device.
  • Advertising and diagnostics data: if ads or related services are enabled, third-party SDKs may collect device identifiers (including advertising IDs where permitted), IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad interactions, app/device information, diagnostic events, and fraud-prevention signals.
  • Support messages: if you contact us, we may receive the information you include in your message and any contact details you provide.

We do not intentionally ask you to provide your real name, precise location, contacts, photos, or other sensitive personal information to play the Game. Do not send sensitive information in a support request.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The Game may display ads provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process data to deliver, measure, personalize (where allowed), and prevent invalid activity associated with ads. The data collected and purposes depend on your region, consent choices, device settings, and the configuration of the advertising SDK.

For details, review Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can manage available advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and any consent options presented in the Game.

3. Unity and other service providers

The Game is built with Unity. Unity technologies or other third-party services may process technical, usage, performance, or diagnostic information when included and enabled in the published version. Their processing is governed by the applicable provider policies and the SDKs integrated into the Game.

Unity privacy information: Unity Privacy Policy.

4. How information is used

Information may be used to operate and maintain the Game, save gameplay progress, deliver and measure advertising, limit ad fraud, diagnose crashes and technical issues, improve stability, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information directly. Third-party advertising providers may process information under their own terms and policies.

5. Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers whose software is included in the Game, such as advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or platform services, if those services are enabled. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the Game, or our legal rights. We do not operate a public profile or social networking feature in the Game.

6. Data retention and storage

Gameplay progress and preferences may be stored locally on your device and generally remain there until you clear the Game’s data or uninstall the Game. Third-party providers determine retention periods for data they collect under their own policies. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to respond and maintain appropriate records.

7. Children’s privacy

The Game is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising features and SDK configurations should be set according to the age rating, applicable law, and Google Play requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can also reset or limit your device’s advertising identifier through Android settings, manage consent where a consent interface is provided, and uninstall the Game to stop further collection by the Game (although this does not itself delete data already retained by third parties).

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. We may need reasonable information to verify and process your request.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the nature of the information we handle. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International processing and third-party links

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their policies and applicable safeguards. Links to third-party websites are provided for convenience; we do not control their content or privacy practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Game, enabled services, or legal requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.
